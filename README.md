# AUTHOR'S SEMANTIC DICTIONARY

A domain-organized concept inventory for conlangers and aspiring authors alike. Every concept is a slot; the language goes in the brackets.

## How To Use This File

Fill each `**[[ ]]**` with your word. In Obsidian the brackets become a link, so `**[[kulaq]]**` creates a note where that lexeme lives — etymology, derivations, example sentences, whatever depth you want. The inventory in ASD.md stays flat and scannable; the notes carry the weight.

## Start Here

Work by **cluster** rather than top-to-bottom. 

Empty slots are a statement, not a failure.

This file is organized for *scannability* and *referencing*. A suggested order to prioritize instead of top-to-bottom:

1. **Pronouns → Personal** — _Grammar & Function Words._ No dialogue happens without them.
2. **Interjections & Formulae** — _Grammar & Function Words._ Greeting, farewell, _ouch_, _hey_. The highest-yield cluster in the file.
3. **Negation & Affirmation** and **Pointing Words** — _Grammar & Function Words._ Yes, no, not, this, that, here, there.
4. **Kinds of Name** — _Speech, Story & Knowledge._ Personal names, place-names and epithets.
5. **Communication** and **Basic Travel** — _Verbs & Actions._ Say, tell, ask, answer; come, go, arrive.
6. **The Base Set** — _Number, Quantity & Collectives._ One through ten.
7. **The Person** and **Sex & Age Categories** — _Identity, Roles & Society._
8. **Immediate Parents**, **The Seniority Split** and **Spouses** — _Kinship & Family._

## Conventions

- **Structure:** `# DOMAIN` → `## SUBDOMAIN` → `### Cluster` → `- CONCEPT : **[[ ]]**`
- **Hypernyms lead.** Where a superordinate exists it is the first entry in its subdomain (ANIMAL before HORSE, PLANT before REED).
- **Parenthetical glosses disambiguate.** `SOLE (OF FOOT)` and `SOLE (OF FOOTWEAR)` are separate concepts that many languages merge.
- **Cross-reference blocks** look like this:

> _Cross-ref: NEAR and FAR are canonical in **SPATIAL LOGIC & ADPOSITIONS**._

A concept has exactly one fillable slot. Where it is relevant in a second domain, a cross-reference points at its home instead of repeating the slot. Fill the canonical location; the pointer keeps the domain readable without splitting the entry in two. Cross-references name domains by title and never by number, so the domains can be reordered without invalidating them.

- **A slash means "these may be one word or several."** For example: `MOSS / LICHEN / FUNGUS`. Break these apart if your language distinguishes, just follow formatting convention of other entries. Or don't, I'm not your dad, do what you want.
